# UM Mechanical Engineering Curriculum

## For future Claude
The course structure of [[Icy Chew Hao Yang]]'s degree: the Bachelor of Mechanical Engineering at [[Universiti Malaya]] (Faculty of Engineering, Department of Mechanical Engineering / JKM). Year 1 and Year 2 are listed in full with credits; Year 2 Semester 2 course codes are confirmed from Icy's own study plan; Year 3 and Year 4 detail is not yet captured at course level (only the credit categories) - to be completed from the official 2025/2026 handbook. Course prefixes: KIX = faculty engineering courses, KIG = mechanical department core. Sources and recency markers are inline. ## Credit structure (as of 2026-06, engine.um.edu.my/bachelor-of-mechanical-engineering)
- Total: 143 credit hours.
  - University courses: 20
  - Faculty core courses: 12
  - Department core courses: 93
  - Department elective courses: 18
- Note: the 2022 intake handbook lists 140 credits (20 University + 18 non-engineering core + 90 engineering core incl. 5 Industrial Training + 12 elective) - the exact split varies by intake year (as of 2022, engine.um.edu.my/ug/haluansiswa2022). Confidence: high (two official sources).
