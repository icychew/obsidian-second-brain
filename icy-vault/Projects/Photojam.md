# Photojam

## For future Claude
Photojam is the youth wing of Persatuan Fotografi Selangor dan KL (a photography society) and one of Icy's biggest community-leadership efforts. This is the umbrella note; each initiative has its own linked project note. Status: active as of 2026-06. Pull this to understand the Photojam program as a whole and to navigate to its sub-projects.

## Overview
- Photojam is the youth arm of Persatuan Fotografi Selangor dan KL.
- [[Icy Chew Hao Yang]] leads or co-leads several initiatives under it, spanning publishing, music, community service, and brand workshops.
- The work mixes creative output (book, song, MV) with community impact (hawker banners) and ecosystem building (camera-brand workshops).

## Initiatives
- [[Photojam Book]] - a published book Icy chief-edited; the society was registered as a publisher; sold to fundraise for Photojam.
- [[Photojam Band and MV]] - an original Photojam theme song and music video; Icy was band manager.
- [[Teratai Market Banners]] - community service designing banners for local hawkers, in collaboration with YB [[Yew Jia Haur]].
- [[Camera Brand Workshops]] - themed workshops run with Fujifilm, Sony, and Nikon.

## Status
- Active as of 2026-06. The book is the current flagship fundraiser (released 2026-03-01).
